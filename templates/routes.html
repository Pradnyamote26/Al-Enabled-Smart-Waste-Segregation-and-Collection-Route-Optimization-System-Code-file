Route Optimization Algorithm Breakdown

1. Nearest Neighbor Algorithm (VRP/TSP)

The system utilizes the Nearest Neighbor Heuristic to solve the Vehicle Routing Problem. Starting from the central depot, the truck iteratively selects the closest unvisited collection point with high priority until all urgent bins are serviced.

2. Haversine Spatial Distance Formula

Geographical distances between GPS coordinates $(\text{Latitude, Longitude})$ are calculated using the Haversine Formula, accounting for the Earth's spherical curvature to ensure precision spatial metrics ($d$ in kilometers).

3. Vehicle Capacity & Priority Weighting

Collection points with fill levels $\ge 60\%$ and priority ratings 4–5 are prioritized first. The algorithm tracks vehicle payload capacity ($1000\text{ kg}$) and schedules mid-route depot return trips if payload limits are exceeded.
